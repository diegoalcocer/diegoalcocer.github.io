About Me

I work in technology and transformation at IDB Invest, in Washington, D.C. My career has crossed unusually different worlds — oil rigs, financial systems, human rights, and now AI — and what connects them is that being wrong has real consequences. That shaped how I build: I own systems end to end, modernize them without breaking what already works, and keep executives and engineers pointed at the same goal.

My focus now is AI: deploying it as a capability institutions can actually trust. I'm trained in both worlds — an MSc in Data Science in progress at Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile, after an MSc in Data Management & Technology Innovation in Barcelona.

How I Work

001
I build for correctness.

Audit trails, validation, and tests are not overhead. On the systems I've built, from treasury accounting to human-rights records, a wrong entry has consequences for real people.

002
I modernize without breaking what works.

Institutions can't stop running while you upgrade them. I've replaced core systems and migrated authentication on live platforms, sequencing change so the lights stay on the whole way through.

003
I treat AI as a capability to govern, not a feature to ship.

AI earns its place when it changes how work gets done and you can measure the result. I build evaluation and guardrails in from the start, because an assistant you can't trust is worse than none.
