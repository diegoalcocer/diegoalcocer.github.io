About Me

I lead technology programs for legal and risk operations at IDB Invest in Washington, D.C. Earlier, I built and modernized systems for the IDB and the OAS, and I started out as a field engineer in the energy industry. In all of it, accuracy mattered, so I'm careful with change, and I keep executives, vendors and engineers working toward the same goal.

I'm completing an MSc in Data Science at Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile, after an MSc in Data Management & Technology Innovation in Barcelona. I work in Spanish and English.
